[orange]Better Flares Headlight Visibility Add-ons

[normal]An add-on for the Better Flares headlight mod. Keeps the DOT 7500K beam shape you already know, and boosts real road visibility in both daytime and nighttime driving.
--------------------
[red]Requires (load ABOVE these in Mod Manager):
[normal]- Better Flares v4.7.3 Base
[normal]- Better Flares v4.7.2 Headlights DOT 7500K
--------------------
[orange]What this changes:
[normal]- Brighter low/hi/front/roof beam color (~1.35x)
[normal]- Longer beam range: low beam +30%, hi/front beam +40%, roof beams +50%
[normal]- Wider beam spread: +5 degrees on every beam stage
[normal]- Stronger near-road glow directly in front of the truck, for better daytime visibility
[normal]- Beam exposure bias raised slightly to help the beam stay visible against bright daylight
--------------------
[orange]What this does NOT change:
[normal]- Beam shape/cutoff pattern (masks untouched)
[normal]- Beam aim/rotation
[normal]- AI traffic headlights
--------------------
